Every part of the padlock
is a part of the thing you are about to build.
The lever The tool that changes the file
The hasp The instant before the tool runs — the only moment your lock gets to speak
The padlock A small script of yours that reads what is about to happen and answers yes or no
The tag The reason, in words the agent reads before it changes course
The key Whatever decides the lock is on, and who is allowed to take it off
